About this collection

This site is a historical atlas of bird specimens held by the three largest Portuguese natural history museums. It covers material collected between 1840 and 1980 — 3,457 records across 338 species and 76 families, from mainland Portugal to Madeira, the Azores and the Selvagens. Specimens collected after 1980 are deliberately excluded, so the maps reflect the historical distribution of the collections rather than data from publisged atlases and present-day occurrence.

Each record corresponds to a preserved specimen with an associated locality. Coordinates were assigned retrospectively from the original label data, so every record carries a coordinate uncertainty in metres. That uncertainty should be taken into account in any spatial analysis: some historical localities are resolved only to the parish or municipality.

Using these data

The records are available as a single GeoJSON file, points.geojson, and as CSV from the Records tab. Fields follow Darwin Core terms where applicable.

Scientific names follow conventional binomial form, with genus derived from the first part of the name. One hundred records carry no collection year; these are listed with an unknown year and are always shown on the map, regardless of how the year slider is set.
