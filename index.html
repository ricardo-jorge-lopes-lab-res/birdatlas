About this collection

This site brings together georeferenced bird specimen records held by three Portuguese university natural history museums. The records span 1840 to 2017 and cover 338 species across 76 families, from mainland Portugal to Madeira, the Azores and the Selvagens.

Each record corresponds to a preserved specimen with an associated locality. Coordinates were assigned retrospectively from the original label data, so every record carries a coordinate uncertainty in metres. That uncertainty should be taken into account in any spatial analysis: some historical localities are resolved only to the parish or municipality.

  • MHNCUP — Museu de História Natural e da Ciência da Universidade do Porto 1,544 records

Using these data

The records are available as a single GeoJSON file, points.geojson, and as CSV from the Records tab. Fields follow Darwin Core terms where applicable.

Ten records carried nonstandard capitalisation in the scientific name — a lowercase genus, or a capitalised specific epithet — which split the same taxon into two entries in the filters. Those names have been regularised to the conventional binomial form, and the original spelling is kept in each record under scientificNameVerbatim. Genus is derived from the first part of the scientific name.
